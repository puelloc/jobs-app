## Departures

**All eleven resolved. No `OutcomeUnverifiableATSTitle`, no 403, no timeout, and no brand-vs-vendor
precedence failure.** Three results are worth a note rather than a fix:

1. **Nike** resolved to `careers.nike.com`, which is the redirect target of the recorded
   `jobs.nike.com`. The gate follows redirects and stores the document actually read, so this
   agrees with the prediction and additionally identifies Workday as the host.
2. **Boeing** resolved to `jobs.boeing.com` rather than the recorded `boeing.com/company/careers`.
   Both are Boeing careers entry points; the scan preferred the link whose text was exactly
   "Careers" and which carried a tracking query string. The rejected candidate
   (`boeing.com/company/careers`) was refused as `generic_title_without_company`, which is a
   **false rejection**: its title is "Careers", and the company token "boeing" is absent from
   that title alone. Treating a bare "Careers" title as generic is too strict when the URL
   path already carries the signal.
3. **Salesforce** resolved to `/company/careers/?bc=HL` rather than `/company/careers/`. A
   tracking parameter is being kept in the stored URL. Harmless for fetching, but it makes two
   spellings of one page look like two candidates on a later run.

## Rate and hygiene

74s for eleven companies, so roughly 6.7s each at the limiter's per-host concurrency of 1
and 500ms floor. That is inside the expected two-minute envelope, which is evidence the limiter
is actually wrapping the fetcher rather than being bypassed.

One artefact worth recording: an earlier attempt was killed at 60s by the harness and left
`scrape_runs` row 4 in `status='running'`. The scheme anticipates this - a stuck `running` row is
how a crash is meant to be visible - but nothing reclaims it, which is the documented non-goal.

This was a **dry run**: attempts and a `dry_run=1` run row were written, and no company was
modified. Every `companies.website` in the eleven is still NULL, which is also why tier 1
fired: `ResolveHomepages` is enabled whenever any selected company lacks a website.
